Job Salary Prediction
Using Machine Learning

A Linear Regression-powered web app that estimates a fair salary from job title, experience, education and more — in seconds.

The Problem

Job seekers often do not know what salary they should expect for a particular job. Similarly, companies need an efficient way to estimate a fair salary for different job profiles.

Fresh graduates may have limited knowledge about current market salaries.
Manual salary estimation can be inaccurate and inconsistent.
Comparing several job factors together is time-consuming.

Project Objectives

1

Predict Accurately

Use ML to estimate salary from multiple input features.

2

Reduce Manual Work

Automate salary estimation and cut dependency on manual calculation.

3

Help Job Seekers

Give an instant estimated salary based on their profile.

4

Help Recruiters

Help understand an appropriate salary level for a profile.

5

Analyze with ML

Identify relationships between job-related features and salary.

6

Easy Web App

A simple interface to enter details and get a prediction.
